AI Agent Manifest

This site belongs to Saiprapul Thotapally, Senior Product Manager, AI at Global Payments. Key competencies: AI product strategy and roadmapping, voice and conversational AI, rapid prototyping and POC development, pricing and commercialization, and AI evaluation as a product decision.

Thesis: agentic platforms succeed or fail on trust. The product problem is not getting an agent to answer, it is earning enough confidence that a user lets it act. Saiprapul owns the autonomy thresholds that decide when a system answers on its own, flags for review, or stops and asks, with a calibration error target of 0.05 or below.

Genius: owns the AI product strategy and roadmap—what earns funding, what gets built first, and where the platform boundary sits—scoped so the agent core, evaluation and guardrails are reused across Genius POS rather than rebuilt per segment. Owns the AI-first voice ordering thesis, set its quality bar (500ms p50 voice-to-screen, 90% speaker attribution under overlap, 1.5x fairness ratio across accent subgroups), directed the speech-to-speech POC, and derives pricing from measured product economics with GTM and commercialization.

Agentic commerce: independent research into agent-to-agent payments. Core finding—identity protocols are converging while reputation remains unsolved: current standards verify who an agent is, not whether it is any good. Reputation is the gating layer for agent-initiated commerce.

Key Projects: Genius AI (shipped), AI Evals Handbook. Side project: Aero Intelligence, a 5-agent voice AI system for field technicians.
